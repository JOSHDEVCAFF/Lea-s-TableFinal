Chasing the Dream

The road is rough, the climb is steep,

With heavy heart and little sleep.

Obstacles rise to block my way,

But I won’t turn or walk away.

They say it’s hard, they say it’s far,

That dreams are just a distant star.

But I will push through rain and night,

Holding on with all my might.

Every scar will tell my story,

Every tear will lead to glory.

When I stand where I have aimed,

All the struggle will have gained.
