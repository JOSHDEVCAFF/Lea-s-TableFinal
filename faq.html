Everything you might want to know before pulling up a chair.

What kinds of food do you serve?

Our menu brings together Japanese favorites like sushi, ramen, and katsu; Chinese classics including dumplings, fried rice, and noodles; and Filipino comfort food such as adobo, sisig, and sinigang.

Can you accommodate dietary needs or allergies?

Some dishes may be adjusted. Please tell us about your dietary needs and allergies before ordering. Our kitchen handles common allergens, so we cannot guarantee an allergen-free meal.

Where do you deliver?

We serve Mandaue City and selected nearby areas in Cebu, subject to availability. We’ll confirm the delivery radius, timing, and any delivery fee when arranging your order.

Do I need a reservation?

For groups, private dining, and catering, getting in touch ahead of time is recommended. Availability depends on the date and group size.

Can I order party trays or catering?

Absolutely. Visit our Culinary Services page and contact us with your date, guest count, and the kind of gathering you’re planning.

Are the checkout and payment pages real?

No. This is a store preview. Adding items and trying the checkout flow are demonstrations only; no real orders or payments are processed.
