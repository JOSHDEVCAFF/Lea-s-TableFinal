Poems About Dreams


Hold Fast to Dreams
"Hold fast to dreams
For if dreams die
Life is a broken-winged bird
That cannot fly."

— Langston Hughes


Dreams and Character
"Dreams are the touchstones of our character.
They show us what we are capableof becoming,
and light the path we are meant to walk."

— Henry David Thoreau


The Beauty of Dreams
"The future belongs to those who believe
in the beauty of their dreams.
Do not follow where the path may lead.
Go inteads wher there is no path
and leave a trail."

— Eleanor Roosevelt
